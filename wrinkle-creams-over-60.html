Wrinkle creams for women over 60 — gentle care for mature skin

Skin in your 60s and beyond benefits most from gentle, consistent care that focuses on comfort, hydration, and barrier health. This guide explains what helps and what is best avoided.

Mature skin in your 60s — what is happening

Skin in the 60s and beyond continues to change in several ways that influence which skincare products are most useful:

  • Thinner-feeling skin: The dermis (the deeper layer of skin) loses thickness over time, which can make skin feel more delicate and look more fragile.
  • Significant dryness: Sebum production continues to decline. Many women find their skin needs much more moisture in their 60s than it did in younger years.
  • Slower healing: Skin can take longer to recover from irritation, dryness, or damage.
  • Increased sensitivity: Products that were tolerated well in earlier decades may cause irritation as skin thins and the barrier becomes more vulnerable.
  • Crepey texture: Thinning skin and loss of elasticity can produce a crepey appearance, particularly on the neck, hands, and arms.

Comfort first

The most important guiding principle for skincare in your 60s is comfort. A routine that feels comfortable, is consistent, and supports the skin barrier will do more for your skin than an aggressive active-ingredient approach that your skin cannot tolerate.

This does not mean giving up on active ingredients entirely. But prioritising barrier health and hydration first creates a foundation that makes everything else work better.

Barrier repair — the priority

A healthy skin barrier is especially important for mature skin. Ceramides are among the most useful ingredients for this purpose. They are gentle, well tolerated, and directly support the barrier's ability to hold moisture and keep out irritants.

Rich cream moisturisers containing ceramides, glycerin, hyaluronic acid, and squalane or shea butter are often ideal for 60s skin.

Gentle routines — less is more

A simple, consistent routine of three or four steps will usually outperform a complex routine applied inconsistently or with products that irritate mature skin.

Morning (simple)

  1. Gentle, hydrating cleanser — or simply water if skin is very dry and was well-cleansed the night before
  2. Rich moisturiser with ceramides and hyaluronic acid
  3. SPF 30–50 — important at every age for ongoing skin protection

Evening (simple)

  1. Gentle cleanser to remove SPF and the day
  2. Rich night cream or moisturiser with ceramides, peptides, or retinol (if tolerated — see below)
  3. Eye cream if desired

Retinol in your 60s — use with care

Retinol can still be appropriate for women in their 60s, but mature skin is often more sensitive to its irritating effects. If you wish to use retinol:

  • Use the lowest available concentration — 0.025% to 0.05%
  • Start once a week at most
  • Mix or buffer with a rich moisturiser
  • Take a break if skin becomes very dry or irritated

Many women in their 60s find that peptides suit them better than retinol and are a comfortable alternative.

Cream texture — why it matters for 60s skin

Lightweight gels and serums that suit oily younger skin are rarely the right choice for mature, dry skin in the 60s. Look for rich cream or balm-like textures. Night creams with an occlusive layer (shea butter, squalane, or similar) can be especially helpful for supporting overnight skin repair.

When to avoid harsh products

  • Avoid high concentrations of AHAs if skin is very dry or thin — they can cause excessive irritation
  • Avoid foaming, gel-based cleansers that strip moisture from already-dry skin
  • Avoid fragrance in products — the risk of irritation is higher in mature skin
  • Avoid using many active ingredients at the same time without guidance
  • Avoid hot water when washing your face — lukewarm water is gentler

SPF — still essential at 60+

Daily SPF remains valuable at every age. UV exposure continues to age the skin and can contribute to pigmentation, skin thinning, and increased skin cancer risk. SPF moisturisers with a rich, comfortable texture are available and well suited to 60s skin.
