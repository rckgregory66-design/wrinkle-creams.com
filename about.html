About us

About wrinkle-creams.com

wrinkle-creams.com exists to help women make better skincare decisions — by understanding what's in products, what the ingredients actually do, and what realistic expectations look like.

Our mission

The skincare industry is vast, often confusing, and frequently misleading. Marketing language is designed to sell, not to inform. Prices bear little relation to quality. Claims range from carefully hedged to outright unsubstantiated.

wrinkle-creams.com was built to cut through that. Our goal is to help women — particularly those approaching or in their 40s, 50s, and 60s — understand what is in wrinkle creams, what the ingredients can realistically do, how to build a consistent routine that suits their skin type and budget, and what to look for and avoid before spending money on products.

We do not shame ageing. We do not use scare tactics. We do not promise overnight results. We explain what the evidence actually says — in plain English.

What we cover

  • Honest ingredient education — retinol, peptides, hyaluronic acid, vitamin C, niacinamide, ceramides, SPF, and more
  • Practical guides for different skin types — dry, oily, sensitive, combination, and mature
  • Age-specific guidance — what changes in your skin in your 40s, 50s, and 60s, and what to prioritise
  • Morning and evening routines that are simple, consistent, and suited to real life
  • Concern-specific guides — fine lines, eye area, forehead, neck, wrinkle prevention
  • Myth-busting — calling out misleading marketing claims clearly and directly
  • Future product reviews — hands-on tested, ingredient-analysed, and honestly assessed

What we do not do

  • We do not publish fake reviews or placeholder product rankings
  • We do not invent expert quotes, clinical study references, or before-and-after results
  • We do not make medical claims or offer personalised medical advice
  • We do not use affiliate links without clear disclosure
  • We do not accept paid placements without disclosing them
  • We do not recommend products we have not properly assessed

Our editorial approach

Content on wrinkle-creams.com is written with the following principles:

  • Facts are checked. Ingredient claims are based on published research, not brand marketing. Where evidence is limited or conflicting, we say so.
  • Claims are proportionate. We use language that matches the strength of the evidence — "may help reduce the appearance of fine lines" when the evidence is moderate; "is well supported by research" only when it is.
  • Limitations are stated. If an ingredient has a limited evidence base, or if results typically take months, we say that clearly.
  • Disclaimers are genuine. We encourage readers to speak to a dermatologist or doctor for personal skin concerns. This is not a legal formality — it is genuinely important.
  • No shaming. Ageing is normal. This site exists to inform choices, not to create anxiety about the way skin changes naturally.

Not medical advice

Everything on wrinkle-creams.com is for general information and education only. It does not constitute medical advice and cannot substitute for personalised guidance from a qualified dermatologist or healthcare professional. If you have a skin concern, a reaction to a product, or a condition affecting your skin, please speak to a qualified professional.

The future of this site

wrinkle-creams.com is being built in phases. The current site is an advice and education resource. Future phases will add hands-on product reviews, side-by-side comparisons, and curated product recommendations — all based on verified testing and honest ingredient analysis.

When affiliate links are added to this site, they will be clearly disclosed on every page where they appear. Affiliate relationships will never influence editorial content or product assessments.
