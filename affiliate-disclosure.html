Affiliate disclosure

This page explains what affiliate links are, how and when they may be used on wrinkle-creams.com, and what our commitments are to readers when they are.

Current status — no affiliate links active

At the time of writing, wrinkle-creams.com does not earn commission from any links on this site. No affiliate links are currently active. This page is published in advance of any affiliate partnerships being established, so readers understand our approach from the outset.

What are affiliate links?

An affiliate link is a tracked link to a retailer or product. When a reader clicks an affiliate link and makes a purchase, the site that provided the link may receive a small commission from the retailer — at no additional cost to the buyer.

Affiliate programmes are used by many independent publishing and review websites as a way to generate revenue that supports the cost of producing content.

How affiliate links may be used on this site in future

When product reviews and product comparison content is added to wrinkle-creams.com, we may use affiliate links to retailers where reviewed products can be purchased. If and when this happens:

  • Every page containing an affiliate link will carry a clear disclosure statement near the top of the page
  • Individual affiliate links will be labelled or noted where practical
  • The fact that a link is affiliate will be stated — not buried in small print

What affiliate relationships will NOT affect

If we enter into affiliate relationships with retailers or brands, those relationships will not influence:

  • Which products we choose to review
  • The content of our reviews or assessments
  • Whether a product receives a positive or negative assessment
  • The ranking or placement of products in comparison tables
  • Our ingredient analysis or factual content

A product that earns us a higher commission will not receive a more positive review. A product that earns us no commission will not be excluded. Editorial standards apply equally to all content, regardless of commercial relationships.

PR samples and gifted products

If products are received as PR samples or gifts for review purposes, this will be stated clearly within the relevant review. Receiving a product free of charge does not guarantee a positive review and does not influence our assessment process.

Sponsored content

If any content on this site is produced in partnership with or paid for by a brand, it will be clearly labelled as sponsored or paid content. Sponsored content will follow the same factual and accuracy standards as editorial content, but readers will always know when a commercial relationship exists.

Pricing accuracy

When product prices are listed, they are verified at the time of writing. Prices can and do change. We note the review date for any priced content. Always check the current price at the retailer before purchasing.

Third-party retailer relationships

Affiliate links point to third-party retailers. We are not responsible for those retailers' pricing, stock availability, returns policies, or the accuracy of their product information. Always check product details directly with the retailer before purchasing.

UK and international readers — regulatory context

This site aims to comply with UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) and Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) guidance on disclosure of affiliate and commercial relationships in online content. Readers outside the UK should note that equivalent regulations apply in their own jurisdiction (e.g. FTC guidelines in the United States).

Questions

If you have any questions about our affiliate relationships or commercial arrangements, please use our contact page. We aim to respond to all genuine enquiries.
