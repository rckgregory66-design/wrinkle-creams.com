How to compare wrinkle creams yourself

Until our product reviews are live, here is how to compare wrinkle creams effectively using the information available on product labels and retailer listings:

  1. Find the INCI ingredients list — usually on the back of packaging or on the retailer listing. The first five ingredients make up the bulk of the formula.
  2. Identify the key active — is it retinol, peptides, vitamin C? How high does it appear in the list? Lower down means a lower concentration.
  3. Check for fragrance — look for "parfum" or "fragrance" in the list. If you have sensitive skin, opt for fragrance-free.
  4. Match texture to your skin type — rich creams for dry skin, gel-creams for oily, fragrance-free for sensitive.
  5. Check the packaging — vitamin C and retinol degrade in light and air. Avoid open jars for these ingredients; prefer tubes or pumps.
  6. Ignore most label claims — focus on the ingredients, not the marketing copy on the front.
  7. Compare price per ml/g — a larger jar may be better value than a small luxury product with the same formulation.
